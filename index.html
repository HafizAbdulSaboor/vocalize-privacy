Privacy Policy

Vocalize · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Vocalize app ("Vocalize", "the app", "we") handles your information. Vocalize is a voice recorder that also turns speech into text. We designed it to keep your data on your device.

1. Information we collect and how it is used

  • Audio recordings. When you record, the audio is saved as a file in the app's private storage on your device. We do not upload recordings to our servers, and we do not have access to them.
  • Transcriptions, names, categories and favourites. Details about your recordings are stored in a local database on your device only.
  • App settings. Preferences such as light/dark theme are stored locally on your device.
  • Account information (Google Sign-In). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo through Firebase Authentication, only to identify your account inside the app. We do not sell or share it.

2. Permissions

  • Microphone is needed to record audio and to transcribe speech. The app records only when you start a recording.
  • Internet is used for Google Sign-In, and for speech recognition and translation features as described below.

3. Third-party services

Vocalize uses the following services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In (Google LLC) – policies.google.com/privacy
  • Speech recognition – uses the speech recognition service built into your device's operating system (on Android this is provided by Google). Depending on your device and settings, audio may be processed by that service. Vocalize does not receive or store that audio on any server of its own.
  • Google ML Kit Translation – translation runs on your device. Language models are downloaded once over the internet; your text is not sent to us.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties, except the service providers listed above that are needed for the features you use. The app contains no advertising and no analytics trackers.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your recordings and related data stay on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app, which removes all locally stored data. To delete your account data held through Google Sign-In, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.

6. Security

Recordings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally access. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

Vocalize is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
